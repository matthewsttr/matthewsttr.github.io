# Differential Equations

A *differential equation* is an equation the derivatives of some unknown function. Examples

$$ \frac{dx}{dt} = x^2 \qquad y'(x) + y^{\prime\prime}(x) = 2 yx\qquad \frac{\partial f}{\partial t} = \nabla f $$
